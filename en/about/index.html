About us

Why we’re building Kolik

We bring the records, time-off requests and internal tasks HR teams work with every day into one place.

When someone requests time off, their manager needs the team calendar and HR needs an up-to-date leave record. When those details live in different places, a simple approval takes longer. Kolik lets everyone work from the same request.

One time-off request, three roles

  1. ✎

    Employee

    Chooses the leave type and dates, then submits the request.

  2. ✓

    Manager

    Checks the team calendar and reviews the request.

  3. ▦

    HR

    Tracks the approval and time-off history in the same record.

How we design Kolik

Start with everyday work
We focus on making common tasks clear, such as inviting an employee, reviewing a request or seeing who has been assigned a piece of equipment.
Use the modules your team needs
One team might start with time off. Another might want employee records, payroll and support requests together.
Keep ownership clear
Employees, managers and HR teams work in views suited to their roles. It’s clear who reviews a request and who approves it.

ABOUT KOLIK

HR’s work.
Everyone’s working day.

Kolik is a modular platform that brings employee records, HR processes and everyday workplace operations into one workspace.

When someone joins, there is more to do than create a profile. Documents need organising, training needs assigning and equipment needs handing over. Kolik brings those tasks into a workspace the people involved can follow.

START WITH THE EVERYDAY

When information is scattered,
work takes longer.

Asking who has the information, looking for the latest file or checking where a request has got to. Small tasks like these add up throughout the day.

In Kolik, employee information, requests and approvals can be followed through their records. Employees can see their own requests, managers can review what needs their attention and HR can access the current record.

WHAT COMES TOGETHER IN KOLIK?

The work around your people.
The needs of your workplace.

Before they join, and after.

Follow job openings, applications and interviews through hiring. Once someone joins, manage their employee record, documents and place in the organisation in the same platform.

Daily schedules, month-end preparation.

Time off, attendance and shifts keep your team’s working arrangements in view. Use payroll and pay tools to review salaries, additional payments and deductions. Track assignments, feedback and goals through learning and performance.

The work around the workplace.

Who has a company laptop, when a vehicle needs maintenance or who owns an open support request. Workplace operations brings equipment, fleet, stock, contracts, documents and shared resources together.

AROUND THE WAY YOUR TEAM WORKS

You don’t have to use
everything at once.

Start with time-off management and add employee records or other modules as you need them. Access for employees, managers and HR is set according to their roles.

Kolik is available on the web, iPhone and Android, in Turkish and English. It can run in the cloud or on your organisation’s servers. Kolik Assistant helps with everyday questions and tasks within each user’s permissions.

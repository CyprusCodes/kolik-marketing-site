Learning

A place to learn.

Organise video lessons and resources into programmes and units. Assign training to people, departments or everyone, with due dates.

What’s included
Courses
Video lessons and resources organised into programmes and units.
Assignments
Training assigned to people, departments or everyone, with due dates.
Progress tracking
Completed and overdue training visible at a glance.
Feedback
Ratings and comments on every unit.

Performance

Feedback to move forward.

Reviewers, questions and weightings are set up in one flow. Feedback comes from the employee, their manager, peers, direct reports and even clients.

What’s included
Guided setup
Reviewers, questions and weightings set up in one flow.
Full 360°
Self, manager, peers, direct reports and clients.
Protected anonymity
Results stay hidden until enough people have responded.
Custom questionnaires
Company competencies with weighted sections and rating scales.

Onboarding

Start with the first week.

Step-by-step guides for IT setup, company policies and benefits. HR keeps the answers to common questions up to date.

What’s included
Onboarding guides
Step-by-step guides for IT setup, policies, benefits and the first week.
Help & FAQ
Answers to common questions, kept up to date by HR.
Welcome training
Onboarding courses assigned to every new joiner.
New joiner progress
Onboarding progress for each new employee in one view.

Goals and results

Keep following through.

Goals are updated with regular check-ins and their status is calculated automatically. When a cycle closes, scores, strengths and PDF reports are ready.

What’s included
KPIs
Targets with regular check-ins and a full progress history.
OKRs
Individual goals connected to team and company objectives.
Live status
On track, behind or at risk, calculated automatically.
PDF reports
A report for every employee, sent to managers in one click.
Detailed results
Scores by area and reviewer group, with strengths and development areas.
Visibility control
Settings for what employees and managers can see.
Review history
Each employee’s results across past cycles.
My Performance
Goals, reviews and results for every employee in one place.

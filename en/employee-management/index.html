How a new hire joins the team

Three steps from invitation to first day, and the profile that’s ready on day one.

  1. 28 Nov, 10:05

    Manager

    The invitation goes out

    Department, job title and start date are filled in, and the welcome message is personalised.

  2. 28 Nov, 14:32

    Employee

    The account is set up

    The new hire creates their account from the email invitation and their status turns “Active”.

  3. 1 Dec

    Team

    First day

    Their personal details, leave balance and documents sit in one profile, and they appear on the org chart under their manager.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new employee?

Create an employee record, select their department and job title, then send the employee an invitation.

Can I customise the welcome message?

Yes. You can edit the welcome message when sending an invitation.

Can I track pending invitations?

Yes. You can see pending invitations from the employee management dashboard.

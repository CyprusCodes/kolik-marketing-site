Recruitment

Keep each candidate’s progress in view.

Candidates move stage by stage from application to offer. Notes, feedback and emails stay on the candidate’s record.

What’s included
Careers page
A branded jobs page that can sit on your company website.
Pipeline board
Candidates move through stages from application to offer.
Candidate database
Full history, notes and feedback for every applicant, plus talent pools.
Automatic emails
Candidates get an update at every stage, in Turkish or English.

AI screening

Context for your shortlist.

Each CV is checked against the job’s requirements and scored out of 100, with the reasons written down. An AI interviewer can run the first round.

What’s included
CV scoring
Every CV scored out of 100 against the job’s requirements, with evidence.
Video interviews
First-round interviews run by AI, with a transcript, strengths and concerns.
Eight languages
Interviews in Turkish, English, Greek and five other languages.
Built-in fairness
Personal details are hidden before the AI reads a CV.

Assessments

Assessments alongside the conversation.

Send candidates or employees a private link. The test is timed and the result is ready the moment it ends.

What’s included
Question bank
Reusable questions in categories, with bulk import from Excel.
Timed and fair
Time limits, random question pools and attempt limits.
No account needed
A private link is all a candidate or employee needs.
Instant results
Scores are calculated automatically and added to the person’s record.

What’s in Kolik

The whole employee journey, from first interview to last working day. Start with the modules you need and add the rest later.

Hire2 modules

  • RecruitmentJob posts, applications and interviews in one pipeline, with AI CV scoring.
  • AssessmentsOnline tests for candidates and employees, sent by link and marked automatically.

Develop2 modules

  • LearningCourses, training assignments and onboarding for new joiners.
  • Performance360° reviews, KPIs and OKRs, and results reports.

People1 module

  • Employee managementProfiles, org chart, documents and offboarding.

Time4 modules

  • Time offRequests, approval chains and leave policies that run on their own.
  • AttendanceEntry and exit records from Hikvision card readers.
  • TimesheetsDaily work logs and overtime for site and field teams.
  • Shift calendarWeekly shift plans by position, with colour-coded shift types.

Pay2 modules

  • PayrollMonthly pay reviewed and sent to the bank as a ready-made file.
  • Timesheet pay runsPay calculated from approved timesheets, with overtime, advances and deductions.

Operate8 modules

  • Fleet managementVehicles, drivers, maintenance and renewal reminders.
  • Asset managementCompany equipment, who holds it and its return history.
  • InventoryStock across locations, barcode scanning and employee requests.
  • ContractsSupplier and service contracts with renewal reminders.
  • Document archiveScanned originals and who currently holds the paper copy.
  • Internal supportIT, facilities and HR requests routed to the right person, with deadlines.
  • Meeting roomsRoom availability, bookings and rooms that need approval.
  • FormsRequests, surveys and sign-ups with conditional questions.

Shared across every module

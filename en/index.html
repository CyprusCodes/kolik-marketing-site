Common questions

Which languages does Kolik support?

The platform, emails and reports are in Turkish and English. Each user picks their own language.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. Employees request leave, open tickets and ask the assistant from the iOS and Android apps. Managers approve from their phones.

Can we run it on our own servers?

Yes. Kolik runs in the Kolik cloud with nothing to install, or on company servers for teams that keep HR data inside their network.

How do we move our existing data?

Employee details and leave history are imported from Excel and checked together. This usually takes a day.

Do we need every module?

No. Start with the modules you need and add more when you are ready.

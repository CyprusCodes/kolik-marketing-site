How a meeting room gets booked

Thursday 3 December. A room is needed for a client visit in the afternoon.

  1. 1

    Manager

    Sets up the rooms

    Decides which rooms can be booked. If there are none, employees are pointed to their manager.

  2. 2

    Employee

    Finds a free slot

    Checks the day and sees the focus room is free from 14:00 to 16:00.

  3. 3

    Employee

    Books it

    Picks the room, date and time. The booking shows in the “Bookings” tab.

  4. 4

    Team

    Sees it on the calendar

    The meeting sits on the same calendar as holidays and approved time off, which can connect to Google Calendar.

Room availability

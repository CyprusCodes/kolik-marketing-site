Internal support

A clear owner for every request.

Each request goes straight to the person responsible, with a response time. Warnings come before a deadline is missed, and managers are alerted when it passes.

What’s included
Automatic routing
Each request sent straight to the person responsible.
Response times
Deadlines on every ticket, with warnings before they are missed.
Escalation
Managers alerted when a deadline passes.
SLA reports
Regular summaries of performance against deadlines.

Asset management

Who has the equipment?

Company equipment, from laptops to phones, is tracked from purchase to return. Its condition is recorded on hand-over and return.

What’s included
Asset register
Cost, supplier, warranty and service dates for every item.
Issue and return
Equipment handed out and returned, with its condition recorded.
Assignment history
A full record of who had what, and when.
Service alerts
Expired warranties and overdue servicing highlighted.

Fleet management

How is the fleet doing?

Vehicles are registered with their assigned driver, insurer and status. Maintenance, inspections and repairs are kept with costs and photos.

What’s included
Vehicle register
Details, assigned driver, insurer and status for every vehicle.
Service and damage
Maintenance, inspections and repairs with costs and photos.
Safety checklists
Regular checks of tyres, brakes, lights and safety equipment.
Renewal reminders
Insurance, inspections, road permits, emissions and licences at 30, 14 and 0 days.

Meeting rooms

Find room for the meeting.

Availability and bookings for every office in one place. Selected rooms, such as the boardroom, need approval; the rest can be booked directly.

What’s included
Availability search
Free rooms found by time, size and equipment.
Easy booking
Rooms booked with attendees from the employee list.
Booking approval
Approval for selected rooms, such as the boardroom.
Room calendars
Each room’s schedule at a glance.
Inventory

Products have codes, categories and low-stock alerts. Deliveries are recorded by scanning barcodes with a phone camera.

  • Product catalogue
  • Barcode scanning
  • Multiple locations
  • Employee requests
Contracts

Each contract’s value, owner, files and key dates are kept together. Reminders arrive months before every end date.

  • Contract records
  • Key dates
  • Renewal reminders
  • Auto-archive
Document archive

Documents are kept in folders with every version. You also know who holds the paper original right now, and when it comes back.

  • Digital archive
  • Paper copy tracking
  • Branch transfers
  • Complete audit trail
Forms

Forms are built from a full set of field types. Questions appear based on earlier answers, and responses are collected in one place.

  • Form builder
  • Smart questions
  • Easy sharing
  • Responses

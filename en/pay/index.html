Payroll

Your month-end process, in order.

Employees are grouped by the bank account they are paid from. Bonuses and deductions are adjusted before anything is sent, then the payment sheet and instruction letter are emailed to the bank.

What’s included
Payroll groups
Employees grouped by the bank account they are paid from.
Review before paying
Bonuses and deductions adjusted before anything is sent.
Bank-ready documents
Payment sheet and instruction letter emailed straight to the bank.
Bank details
Employees keep their own bank account details up to date.
Salary history
Every finalised run is saved to the employee’s salary history.

  1. Prepare

    Choose the payment group and period.

  2. Review

    Check salaries, bonuses and deductions.

  3. Send

    Generate the payment list and bank documents.

Timesheet pay runs

From approved hours to pay.

Days worked, paid leave, overtime, allowances and deductions come together in one calculation. Advances are repaid in instalments automatically.

What’s included
Automatic calculation
Days, paid leave, overtime, allowances and deductions in one calculation.
Salary advances
Advances repaid automatically in instalments.
Built-in checks
Warnings for missing salaries, negative pay and clashes with leave.
Bank and cash
Pay split between transfer and cash, with a bank file ready to send.

Records and org chart

What’s included
Employee profile
Personal details, career, salary, bank details and documents together.
Custom fields
Extra fields for anything the company needs to track.
Org chart
The company structure in table, card and org chart views.
Flexible structure
Branches, departments and teams on as many levels as needed.

Joiners and changes

Joining the team

A whole team comes in from a single Excel file. New joiners are invited with their role, manager and start date.

What’s included
Excel import
A whole team imported from a single spreadsheet.
Email invitations
New joiners invited with their role, manager and start date.
Career history

Bulk changes

Documents

During employment

Passports, work permits and certificates are filed on each employee’s profile. Reminders go out on their own as expiry gets close.

What’s included
Document storage
Passports, permits and certificates filed on each profile.
Expiry reminders
Emails 30 days and 14 days before expiry, and on the day.
Employment contracts
Contract types and end dates tracked for every employee.
Reminder settings
A choice of which reminders go out, and to whom.

Offboarding

Moving on

Every item, task and access linked to a leaver shows on one list and is closed before their last day.

What’s included
Company items returned
Equipment marked as returned or reassigned, and vehicles handed over.
Work passed on
Open tickets, contracts, archive documents and pending approvals transferred.
Leave and reviews settled
Leave balance settled, open reviews and tests closed.
Access switched off
Active Directory access disabled in the same step.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a new employee?

Create an employee record, select their department and job title, then send the employee an invitation.

Can I customise the welcome message?

Yes. You can edit the welcome message when sending an invitation.

Can I track pending invitations?

Yes. You can see pending invitations from the employee management dashboard.

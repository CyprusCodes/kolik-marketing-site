Kolik Assistant

Start with a question.

Checking a leave balance, booking a room, adding a task or updating a goal happens in a chat. The assistant works with the user’s own access.

What’s included
Leave
Balances checked and requests made in a sentence.
Meeting rooms
Free rooms found and booked in conversation.
People and tasks
Colleagues found and to-dos created instantly.
Performance and hiring
Goal updates, review reminders and candidate rankings.

Mobile app

Leave balance, new requests, approvals and who’s away, from the phone. The app is in English and Turkish.

What’s included
Leave balance
Remaining, used and pending days at a glance.
Requests
Leave requested in a few taps.
Approvals
Requests approved with a swipe.
Team and reviews
Who is away, and reviews to complete.

Notifications, broadcasts and calendar

From personal alerts to company announcements, each message goes by the right channel and you can see who received it. Holidays and leave share one calendar, synced with Google and Outlook.

What’s included
Notifications
In-app and email alerts, with personal preferences.
Broadcast
Announcements by email, SMS or push, with delivery tracking.
Celebrations
Automatic birthday and work anniversary messages.
Shared calendar
Holidays and leave in one calendar, synced with Google and Outlook.

Reports

Columns, filters and totals chosen in a few clicks. There are 21 data sources, from employees and leave to fleet and tickets.

What’s included
Report builder
Columns, filters and totals chosen in a few clicks.
Every module covered
21 data sources, from employees to fleet and tickets.
Ready-made reports
16 reports ready to run straight away.
Export
Saved, re-run and exported to Excel or CSV.

Roles and permissions

Set per role and recorded in a log. Access can be scoped to a person’s own records, their direct reports, a unit or everyone.

What’s included
Roles
8 ready-made roles, plus custom roles for any team.
Precise permissions
More than 90 permissions, scoped to own records, direct reports, a unit or everyone.
Activity log
Every change and login recorded.
Company settings
Self-service rules, group companies, bank accounts, email and SMS.

Active Directory

Employees are matched to their Active Directory accounts. Accounts are enabled and disabled from Kolik, and access is removed when someone leaves.

What’s included
Account linking
Employees matched to their Active Directory accounts.
Status sync
Account status kept in step with Active Directory.
Access control
Accounts enabled or disabled directly from Kolik.
Offboarding
Access removed automatically when an employee leaves.

Automation

Leave calculations, stuck approvals, celebrations and expiry reminders run every day on their own. Ticket deadlines and card reader data are watched all day.

What’s included
Leave calculations
Accrual, carry-over and expiry processed every night.
Stuck approvals
Requests move up the chain when an approver is away.
Reminders
Emails for documents, fleet and contract deadlines.
All day
Ticket deadlines, Hikvision data and access rules.

Cloud or on-premise

Use Kolik in the cloud with nothing to install, or install it on company servers to keep HR data inside your network.

Languages

Turkish and English

Multi-currency

Flexible leave rules

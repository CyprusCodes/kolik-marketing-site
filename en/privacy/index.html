Privacy Policy

Introduction

Kolik is a human resources management platform that enables organizations to manage employee data, attendance, payroll, and scheduling, with optional integration to Google Calendar for event synchronization.

This Privacy Policy explains how Akdemy Teknoloji (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal data processed through Kolik (the “Platform”), operated under https://kolik.co.

By using Kolik, you (“Client Organization” or “User”) agree to this Privacy Policy and our handling of data as described below.

Who We Are

Akdemy Teknoloji is a technology company headquartered in Nicosia, Northern Cyprus. We provide the Kolik platform to help organizations manage HR processes, including employee data, leave tracking, and payroll management.

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact:
📧 info@kolik.co

Data We Process

Kolik processes information necessary for human resources operations. This includes, but is not limited to:

  1. Full name, email address, and phone number

  2. Employment details (position, department, contract information)

  3. Attendance and leave records

  4. Payroll and bank account details

We do not collect or process personal data for our own marketing or analytics purposes.
All data processed within Kolik belongs to the Client Organization, and Akdemy Teknoloji acts as a data processor on their behalf.

Purpose of Processing

Data processed in Kolik is used exclusively for organizational HR management, including:

  • Managing employee profiles, contracts, and attendance

  • Processing leave requests and approvals

  • Generating payroll and payment instructions

  • Providing technical support to client organizations

  • Maintaining secure and efficient platform operations

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process data under the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of a contract: To provide HR services to our client organizations.

  • Legal obligations: To comply with employment or data protection laws where applicable.

  • Legitimate interests: For system maintenance, support, and improvement, without infringing on individual privacy.

Use of Google User Data

Kolik integrates with Google Calendar to allow users to view, create, and manage calendar events directly within the Kolik platform. When you connect your Google Account, we request access to your Google Calendar data through the Google OAuth 2.0 process.


Scope requested:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar — See, edit, share, and permanently delete all the calendars you can access using Google Calendar.


How We Use This Data

  • To retrieve and display your Google Calendar events directly from Google’s servers in real time within the Kolik dashboard

  • To allow you to create, update, or delete events in your Google Calendar via the Kolik interface

  • To synchronize these actions instantly with your Google account


Data Storage and Retention

Kolik does not store any Google Calendar event data on its servers.

  • All event data is fetched dynamically from Google via the official Calendar API during an active user session.

  • Only secure OAuth access tokens are stored to enable authorized API communication on your behalf.

  • These tokens are encrypted, stored securely, and automatically invalidated when you revoke access or disconnect your Google Account.


Data Sharing

  • We do not share, sell, or transmit Google user data to any third party.

  • Access to Google APIs is used solely to provide the requested calendar synchronization functionality within Kolik.


Revoking Access

You can revoke Kolik’s access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page.

Once access is revoked, Kolik will no longer be able to retrieve or modify your Google Calendar data.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal and organizational data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. This includes secure HTTPS transmission, encrypted token storage, and strict access controls.

User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data

  • Restrict or object to processing

  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@kolik.co.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or integrations. If we make significant updates, we will notify users via the Kolik platform or email before such changes take effect.

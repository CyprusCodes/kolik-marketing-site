One time-off request, start to finish

Here’s how a time-off request moves through Kolik, step by step, using a sample employee.

  1. Employee

    Asks for Thursday afternoon off

    They pick the leave type and the hours. The form shows their remaining balance and how much this request will use straight away. A few hours off needs no extra steps.

  2. Manager

    The request reaches the manager

    Requests follow the approval steps your company sets up. Who sent it and who approved it, and when, stays in the request’s approval history.

  3. Employee

    Gets notified of the result

    When the request is answered, the employee gets a notification. No need to chase anyone for an update.

  4. Team

    The time off shows on the team calendar

    Approved time off appears on the team calendar alongside public holidays, so the manager and teammates can see who’s out before they plan.

Frequently asked questions

Can employees see their leave balance?

Yes. Employees can check their time-off records and remaining balances in Kolik.

Where do managers review requests?

Pending requests are visible in the time-off dashboard.

Can employees take a few hours off?

Yes. Pick a start and end time to request a few hours off during the day. The amount to deduct is calculated automatically.

TIME OFF

A clear path from request to decision.

  1. Request

    Employees choose their leave type and dates.

  2. Review

    Managers review the request alongside the team calendar.

  3. Follow up

    Employees and HR can see the outcome in the leave record.

What’s included
Quick requests
Full days, half days or hours, with weekends and holidays excluded.
Approval chains
Multi-step approvals that can differ by department or person.
Auto-forwarding
Requests move up the chain when an approver is away.
Daily absence email
A morning list of who is off, sent to managers.

LEAVE POLICIES

Your rules. Consistent leave balances.

Define accrual, carryover and service-based rules. Assign policies to your company, departments or individuals; follow balances and recorded changes.

What’s included
Assigned anywhere
Policies assigned to the whole company, departments or specific people.
Balances kept accurate
Accrual, carry-over and expiry run automatically, with every change recorded.
Seniority and age rules
Extra days by years of service, and minimums by age.
Half-day holidays
Local holidays and half days set up in the calendar.
Entitlement and accrual
Yearly, monthly or per-event entitlement, given upfront or accrued monthly.
Carry-over
None, capped or full, with an optional expiry date.
New joiners
A waiting period and a pro-rated first year.

Attendance

From card reader to employee record

Hikvision entry and exit records help you follow attendance and exceptions.

What’s included
Hikvision integration
Card and keyfob entries synced automatically throughout the day.
Movement history
Every entry and exit shown on the employee profile.
Exceptions flagged
Late arrivals, early departures and absences highlighted.
Attendance reports
Attendance by person, department and branch.
Automatic access switch-off
Active Directory account and building keyfob disabled when rules are met.
IT stays in control
Kolik only switches access off; reactivation stays with IT.
Access rules
Late arrival, early departure, no entry all day, approved leave or suspension.
Exceptions and approvals
Named people excluded; manual actions approved by department.

Frequently asked questions

Can employees see their leave balance?

Yes. Employees can check their time-off records and remaining balances in Kolik.

Where do managers review requests?

Pending requests are visible in the time-off dashboard.

Can employees take a few hours off?

Yes. Pick a start and end time to request a few hours off during the day. The amount to deduct is calculated automatically.
